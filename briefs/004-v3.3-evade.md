# Brief 004 — v3.3: Evade and tackle at hex cost

| | |
|---|---|
| Rule set | v3.3 (new preset, copied from v3.2) |
| Date | 18 Nov 2026 |
| Goal | Light units can strike and get away, and breaking contact costs what a step costs, so Light units stop dying in place after every attack. |
| Branch | `build/004-v3.3-evade` |
| Flags | F21 |

## Context
Light units die far too often (removed in 60–67% of bot games even under the cautious bots). The main reason: an attack ends a unit's turn, so a Light unit that strikes is left standing next to its target and gets counter-attacked with little Dodge. The Editor approved two changes for playtest (Game Laws 6.5 and 6.6):
- **Evade (Light class ability):** after attacking from an adjacent hex, a Light unit may keep moving away.
- **Tackle at hex cost:** breaking contact with an enemy Melee unit costs the leaving unit its own cost for one hex (Heavy 1, Light 0.5), not a flat 1.

## Scope
In: a new preset v3.3 with three switches, the board showing Evade moves, the bot using Evade, tests, a baseline. Out: Cavalry, special attacks, any change to Dodge or HP.

## What to build
1. **New preset `v3.3`**, a copy of `v3.2`, last in `PRESETS` so it's the new-game default. Note: "v3.2 plus: Evade for Light units; tackle costs the leaving unit's hex cost."
2. **Switch `evade`** (on in v3.3). After a Light unit attacks an enemy **in an adjacent hex** and the target **survives**:
   - the unit may keep moving instead of its turn ending;
   - **every step must take it further from the unit it attacked** (hex distance strictly greater after each step than before it);
   - it may use its **remaining movement, Stamina and Command**: each step costs its normal hex cost (Light 0.5), counts toward its movement cap for the turn (4 hexes, including hexes moved before the attack), and the turn's Command;
   - it may leave an enemy zone (Mark) it entered this turn, but stops on entering a new one;
   - breaking tackle while evading costs the tackle cost as normal (item 3);
   - after evading it may not attack again, brace, pull or swap this turn.
   No Evade when: the attack was from 2 or more hexes away (Reach, or a Light Ranged shot from range); the attack removed the target (the attacker takes the hex as now, Article 3.6, and its turn ends); the unit is Heavy.
3. **Switch `tackleHexCost`** (on in v3.3). Leaving contact with an enemy Melee unit costs the leaving unit **its own hex cost** extra (Heavy 1, Light 0.5), once a turn as now, instead of the flat `tackleCost`.
4. **Switch `tackleUsesCap`** (on in v3.3). That extra tackle cost also uses up **one hex of the unit's movement cap**. The Editor leans this way; the Report must also give the baseline with it off (see Report back), so he can compare.
5. **The board.** After a Light unit's adjacent attack that didn't kill, the unit stays selected and its legal Evade hexes show as move hexes. "End unit" (or selecting another unit) ends it without evading. The attack preview says "Evade after: up to N hexes" when Evade will be available. Everything shown comes from the engine (`options`), never computed separately.
6. **Close F21** in `FLAGS.md`: the Editor ruled on 17 Nov that the display stays as it is (the flag keeps the last half point, the holder shows 0, and the next hit of any size brings the flag down). No marker. Game Laws 4.7.

## Rules it touches
Taking the hex after a kill (no Evade after a kill); the hard-stop zone (Evade may leave a zone entered this turn); tackle (cost, once a turn, nobody pays if the Melee unit moves away); set spear (an Evade step that enters a hex next to a braced Heavy Anti-Cavalry counts as entering: it triggers set spear if that is the first entry that turn); Ranged half Strength when adjacent (a Light Ranged attacking from an adjacent hex then evades); the flag holder (a Light holder that attacks from its flag may Evade off it; the flag then drops to its own HP, as when any holder leaves); movement cap; Command.

## Bot minimum
The bot uses Evade. In 200 seeded games of v3.3 (Kit B vs Kit B, balanced lineups, 100 per format), report: how often a Light unit evaded after an eligible attack, and how often it chose not to; Light units removed per game on v3.2 vs v3.3. The bot looks ahead through the engine, so it should pick up Evade without new weights; if it never or always evades, adjust its scoring and say how. Met when the bot evades in a clear majority of eligible attacks where a safer hex exists.

## Must not change
match3, v1, v2, v3, v3.1 and v3.2 play and replay identically (same `regress_engine.js` hashes).

## Checks before opening the pull request
- Every check in `README.md`, plus a new `board/tests/test_v33.js` with a test for every bullet in items 2–4. Each must fail on v3.2 and pass on v3.3.
- The baseline on v3.3 (same batches as before) with the usual table, plus Light units removed per game.

## The Editor's hands-on check
One game on the play link using two or more Light units. Check: after a Light unit hits without killing, you can step it away; it can't step closer; it can't step after a kill.

## Report back
The standard Report. The baseline table v3.2 vs v3.3, with an extra row for Light units removed per game, and one extra 7v7 run (200 games) with `tackleUsesCap` off, so the Editor can see what counting the tackle hex against the cap changes.

- **What's in:**

  - **`evade`:** after an adjacent attack that leaves the target standing, a Light unit isn't done. It may move, and only to hexes where every step is further from the unit it attacked, using its remaining cap, Stamina and Command. Its first evade move may leave a zone it entered this turn; entering a new zone stops it. Breaking tackle costs as normal. While evading it can't attack, brace, pull or swap, and can't be swapped with. End unit, or any other unit acting, ends the evade. There's no Evade after a Reach attack or a shot from range, after a kill (the unit takes the hex and is done), or for a Heavy unit. The log says "may evade" and "evading".
  - **`tackleHexCost`:** breaking tackle costs the leaving unit its own hex cost (Light 0.5, Heavy 1), once a turn as before.
  - **`tackleUsesCap`:** breaking tackle also uses one hex of the cap, for moves and for swaps.

Picking another unit records an End unit for the evader first, so a saved match replays exactly as played. The Rules screen lists the three switches.

- **Anything beyond the brief, and why:**
  1. **Bot change.** Evade is now or never: another unit acting ends it. The bot used to weigh the Evade step against every other unit's options, so it often let another unit act first and lost the Evade. Now, while one of its units may evade, it decides that first: step to its best hex if that scores better than staying, otherwise End unit. Nothing else in the bot changed. Only a unit that may evade triggers this, so older rule sets play exactly as before; the bot-driven regression hashes are unchanged.
  2. **`test_v32.js`** asserted "v3.2 is the last preset". It now checks v3.2 is still offered behind the newest. The newest-preset check is in `test_v33.js`.
  3. **`baseline.js`** gained counters (Light units removed; Evade chances, taken, no hex available; and "safer": a chance where some Evade hex lowers the enemy's expected damage next turn, by the bot's own threat measure). It also gained an optional switch override for the `tackleUsesCap` off run. The random numbers are unchanged: the v3.2 rerun matches the stored v3.2 figures game for game.
  4. **The `evade` card flag** is new data: Evade belongs to the three Light cards, not to a code rule.
  5. **Set spear** only fires where a move ends, as for any move. Zone of Control already stops a unit on the first hex next to an Anti-Cavalry (its zone), so an Evade step can't pass through one. Tested.

  **What it shows:** v3.3 doesn't reduce Light losses in bot games. Every difference in the tables is a handful of games out of 400 (or 200), within what chance alone would produce, and the 5v5 holder-KO rise (96 to 110) is about 1.4 standard errors. Counting the tackle hex against the cap changes little either way. **The goal of the brief is not met in simulation** (F22). Likely cause, not isolated: in 39% of eligible attacks the Light unit had no legal hex to evade to (747 of 1,913 across both formats). When it does evade, it has 1–2 hexes left after moving to the target, still inside enemy reach.
- **Bot minimum (met / not met, with numbers):** **Met, narrowly.** By the brief's definition (200 games, 100 per format, batches 0–4): where a safer hex existed, the bot evaded in 45 of 73 chances at 7v7 (62%) and 9 of 11 at 5v5 (82%), so 54 of 84 (64%). Over all 800 baseline games: 250 of 399 (63%, roughly 58–68% allowing for chance). Counting every chance, including ones where no hex is safer, it evaded 377 of 994 times (38%); staying put there is reasonable. Without the bot change it evaded about 30% of all chances in a 120-game sample, so the change matters. It's a majority, though not overwhelming: the bot weighs staying engaged (pressure on the enemy) against safety.

  3. Set spear checks only where a move ends, as for every move (see beyond the brief, item 5).
